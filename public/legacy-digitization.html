West Bengal Directorate of Health Services — SBHI SOP Case-I Guidelines

Path A: Intact Physical Certificate

1. Operator must physically inspect handwritten certificate for watermark, volume, and seal of Bagula II GP.

2. Mandatory file size cap: Certificate scan < 100 KB, Mother's Aadhaar < 50 KB.

3. Generate Form A summary sheet and instruct citizen to present physical copy to the Sub-Registrar.

Path B: Lost / Damaged Certificate

1. Citizen must produce a valid Police General Diary (GD) report from Hanskhali Police Station.

2. Non-Availability search is activated against paper registers.

3. Secondary age proof (Class-X Admit/Marksheet) is mandatory to prevent identity manipulation.

May 2026 State Re-Verification Mandate

Under the active re-verification drive, all manual certificates must be physically matched against original Volume Registers. Once verified, the Sub-Registrar signs the digital certificate with e-Sign on the Janma-Mrityu Tathya Portal (janmamrityutathya.wb.gov.in).
