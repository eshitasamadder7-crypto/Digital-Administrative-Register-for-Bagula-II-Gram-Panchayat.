# Bagula II Gram Panchayat Digital Administrative Register

A comprehensive Digital Administrative Register (DAR) and Citizen Service Portal developed for **Bagula II Gram Panchayat** to digitize administrative workflows, improve transparency, strengthen auditability, and modernize citizen services.

This project is designed as a long-term Government-grade administrative platform rather than a simple certificate generation system.

---

## Development Roadmap

See the detailed progress and feature checklist in our [Development Roadmap](docs/DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md).

## Vision

To create a secure, transparent, scalable and future-proof e-Governance platform that enables digital management of Panchayat administrative records while maintaining compatibility with existing Government office procedures.

The system follows real-world administrative workflows including physical signatures, memo registration, approval hierarchy, digital verification and complete audit trails.
